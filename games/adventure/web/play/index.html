Adventure

Two characters, one player. Move with WASD or arrows; press Q to switch between Jumper and Strong. Space jumps: Jumper reaches higher platforms than Strong. Practice landing on the ledges; cooperative puzzles follow in later work.

P pauses/resumes. Q switches characters; release held movement before steering the newly selected character. Escape releases canvas focus and pauses. N steps while paused. R restarts. Replay advances the same simulation one fixed tick at a time. Focus loss pauses until you explicitly resume. Space jumps on a fresh press while grounded. E is reserved for the later push ability. Recommended viewport: 960×540 or larger. The scene retains a 16:9 composition. 

Ready. Requires WebGPU or WebGL2 with floating-point color attachments.
